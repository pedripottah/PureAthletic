**Status:** Draft template; approval and local adaptation required before use  
**Applies to:** Phase 0 adult discovery and prototype-usability sessions only  
**Last reviewed:** 1 September 2026

This pack provides the participant-facing information and consent record needed
by the [research preparation guide](research-preparation.md). It is not a
privacy notice, legal opinion, safeguarding approval, or permission to recruit
children. The research lead must complete and approve every field below for the
chosen jurisdiction and storage process before recruitment.

## Participant information

### Why are we running this research?

PureAthletic is an early football-planning prototype. We are studying whether
adults can understand what it recommends, why a plan changes, and when someone
should not follow an automated suggestion. The session evaluates the
prototype, not the participant or their athletic ability.

The prototype is not a medical service or an approved training programme. It
must not be used to diagnose a condition, make a return-to-play decision, or
direct real training during this research.

### Why have I been invited?

You are being invited as an adult with a football player/coach or
parent/guardian perspective. Participation does not authorize research with a
child, and no child should attend or supply information for the session.

### What will happen?

The session will take approximately 60–75 minutes. The researcher will:

1. ask about a recent training-planning decision;
2. give you a fictional profile in the local prototype;
3. ask you to complete setup, planning, readiness, logging, progress, and data
   control tasks while thinking aloud; and
4. ask short questions about clarity, safety wording, and accessibility.

You will not be asked to exercise, test pain, reveal a medical history, or
follow a prototype workout. Use only fictional information in the prototype.

### Is participation voluntary?

Yes. You may skip a question, pause, or stop without giving a reason or losing
any agreed compensation. You may request withdrawal using the method and
deadline in the completed release-gate record. The researcher must explain any
point after which fully anonymised, aggregated findings can no longer be
separated.

### What information will be collected?

The study uses a session ID rather than your name in research notes. It may
record your broad participant perspective, device/browser, tasks attempted,
observed actions, usability or accessibility barriers, and answers to the
approved session questions.

Scheduling contact details and the signed consent record must be stored
separately from session notes. The study must not collect exact birth dates,
medical histories, precise locations, team or club names, or identifiable
performance data. Audio, video, photographs, or screen recording are excluded
unless separately described and approved before consent.

If you volunteer sensitive information, the researcher will not ask for more
detail and will follow the approved safeguarding process where necessary.

### How will information be used and protected?

Access, storage, retention, deletion, and withdrawal details are stated in the
completed release-gate record. Repository documents may contain only checked,
anonymised findings; names, contacts, consent records, raw notes, and sensitive
disclosures must not be committed.

Small combinations of role, location, club, age, device, or quotations can
identify someone. The researcher will remove or generalise those details before
sharing or committing a synthesis.

### What are the possible risks and benefits?

There is no promised personal benefit. Feedback may improve the prototype.
Possible discomfort includes discussing planning difficulties, seeing
pain-related warnings, encountering an inaccessible interface, or feeling that
the prototype is evaluating you. You may skip or stop at any time. Report any
concern using the completed safeguarding or complaints contact.

### Questions or concerns

Ask questions before deciding. Use the completed participant, privacy, or
safeguarding contact above for questions, withdrawal, complaints, or data
requests.

## Adult consent record

The participant should receive the completed information section and have time
to ask questions before recording consent. Store the completed record privately
and separately from session notes; never commit it to this repository.

```text
Participant ID:
Information-pack version received:
Date:

[ ] I confirm that I am an adult and am participating only from an adult
    player/coach or parent/guardian perspective.
[ ] I have read the participant information and had an opportunity to ask
    questions.
[ ] I understand participation is voluntary and I may skip questions or stop.
[ ] I understand the prototype is not medical or training advice and that I
    must not follow its workouts during this research.
[ ] I understand that I must use a fictional profile and will not provide a
    child's or another person's personal information.
[ ] I understand what information will be collected, who can access it, how
    long it will be kept, and how to request withdrawal.
[ ] I understand that checked, anonymised findings may be used to improve the
    product and may be included in repository documentation.
[ ] I know how to raise a privacy, safeguarding, or research concern.
[ ] I voluntarily agree to participate in this adult research session.

Participant confirmation method:
Participant date/time:
Researcher confirmation:
Researcher date/time:
Consent withdrawn (if applicable):
Withdrawal action and date:
```

Consent is an ongoing process. Reconfirm it if the protocol, prototype task,
recording method, data use, or retention terms change.
