# PureAthletic — Research Round Summary

**Status:** Anonymised synthesis template for completed research rounds
**Last reviewed:** 24 August 2026

Use this template after the individual session forms have been completed and
stored privately. It records evidence and product decisions, not participant
identities, consent records, contact details, medical information, or raw
session notes.

Do not commit a completed copy until it has been checked for indirect
identifiers and sensitive disclosures. Small samples can make combinations of
role, location, club, age, device, and quotation identifying even when names
are removed.

## Round decision

Select exactly one outcome:

- [ ] **Continue** — evidence supports the current question and the next
  evidence-gated step.
- [ ] **Narrow** — continue only with the cohort, problem, or scope stated
  below.
- [ ] **Investigate** — no implementation expansion until the named evidence
  gap is resolved.
- [ ] **Stop** — the evidence does not justify continued product investment in
  the current direction.
