# PureAthletic — Research Preparation Pack

This document turns the Phase 0 research plan into a small, executable first
round. It is preparation guidance, not approval for junior recruitment or
production use.

## First round

Run adult sessions first using seeded or fictional profiles and the local
prototype. Recruit:

- 2–3 adult football players or coaches;
- 2–3 parents or guardians; and
- 1 qualified youth-sport practitioner for a separate review.

Participants should be comfortable discussing how they plan training, schedule
football commitments, and decide when to rest. Do not recruit anyone who would
need to perform exercise or follow an app recommendation during the session.

## Session procedure

1. Explain that this is prototype research, not medical or performance advice.
2. Confirm consent before recording notes; use a session ID instead of a name.
3. Ask about a recent planning decision before showing the prototype.
4. Run the tasks in `docs/research-plan.md` without coaching the participant.
5. Record first action, hesitation, help requested, misunderstanding, and final
   outcome for each task.
6. Ask the short questions in `docs/research-session-form.md`.
7. End by explaining the prototype limitations and how notes will be deleted.

## Participant safeguards

- Collect only contact details needed for scheduling and broad participant
  category/device information needed to interpret results.
- Do not collect exact birth dates, medical histories, precise locations, team
  names, or identifiable performance data.
- Do not ask anyone to exercise, test pain, or follow a recommendation.
- Stop and escalate through the approved safeguarding process if someone
  reports harm, distress, or a current safety concern.
- Store notes privately under the session ID and set a deletion date before
  recruitment. Do not commit identifiable notes to the repository.

## Consent checklist

Before each session, confirm that the participant understands:

- participation is voluntary and can stop at any time;
- the session evaluates the prototype, not the participant;
- the prototype is not medical advice and must not be followed as a training
  instruction during research;
- notes will be anonymised, access-limited, and deleted on the stated date;
- anonymised findings may lead to product changes; and
- questions or safeguarding concerns can be raised privately.

For any future child session, obtain jurisdiction-appropriate guardian consent
and child assent through an approved process before recruitment. This document
does not authorize child sessions.

## Minimum evidence to keep

For each session, retain only the completed session form, anonymised findings,
severity, evidence strength, and a concrete backlog decision. After the round,
summarise:

- at least five completed adult sessions across relevant roles;
- the top five usability or safety findings and their dispositions;
- any accessibility barriers;
- unresolved or release-blocking misunderstandings; and
- the decision to continue, narrow, investigate, or stop.
